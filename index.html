I work on ocean waves, numerical methods and machine learning. My DPhil in Engineering Science at the University of Oxford was on data-driven modelling of nonlinear surface waves, including machine-learning closures for wave breaking in envelope-type wave models. I have since worked on optimal control of tidal range schemes and on wave–current modelling for offshore renewable energy, and more recently on attention mechanisms for sequence models.

This site collects my notes and papers.
